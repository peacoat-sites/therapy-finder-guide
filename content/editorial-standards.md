# How Therapy Finder Guide Is Written

Therapy Finder Guide helps people find and afford mental health care: types of therapy, finding a provider, insurance and costs, and what to expect. This page explains, plainly, how the articles are made.

## How articles are made

Articles on Therapy Finder Guide are drafted with AI writing tools. Each draft starts from public reference sources on the topic, including those listed below, and from recent news when a topic is changing.

Before an article is published, a second automated pass reviews it and removes or rewrites anything it cannot stand behind: statistics, prices or dates it is not confident are correct, studies or organizations that may be misattributed, and personal stories presented as if they happened to the writer.

Articles are not reviewed by a licensed mental health professional before publication. Use them as background for a conversation with one, not as a substitute.

The site owner audits published articles in batches, merging pages that overlap, deleting weak ones and correcting errors.

## Who writes here

The bylines on Therapy Finder Guide (Alex Morgan, Chris Peterson, Jamie Sullivan, Kim Davis, Taylor Brooks) are pen names, one for each area of coverage. They are not real individuals, they hold no professional licenses, and their profile images are illustrations, not photographs of real people.

## Sources we rely on

- [National Institute of Mental Health](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/): Mental-health research
- [SAMHSA](https://www.samhsa.gov/): Treatment and support services
- [American Psychological Association](https://www.apa.org/): Psychology resources
- [988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org/): 24/7 crisis support

Where an article depends on a specific figure, rule or study, it links to or names the source so you can check it.

## Limits of this site

This site is not therapy and cannot assess your situation. If you are thinking about suicide or are in crisis, call or text 988 to reach the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline in the US, or call 911 if you are in immediate danger. For a diagnosis or a treatment plan, see a licensed mental health professional.

## Corrections

If you find an error, please report it through the [contact page](/contact/). Reports go directly to the site owner, and confirmed errors are corrected in the article.

## How the site makes money

Therapy Finder Guide may earn money from display advertising and from affiliate links, including Amazon links (as an Amazon Associate, the site earns from qualifying purchases). Advertisers and affiliate partners do not see or approve articles before they are published.
