Affordable Therapy Options

📅
Open Path Collective: $30–$80/session for income-qualified individuals and families. Searchable directory of therapists accepting reduced rates.
🏠
Community Mental Health Centers: Sliding scale fees based on income. Often serve uninsured and underinsured clients.
🏫
University Training Clinics: Therapy provided by supervised graduate students for $0–$20/session. High-quality care at minimal cost.
📱
Online Platforms (BetterHelp / Talkspace): Subscription-based therapy at $60–$100/week. Convenient and often more affordable than in-office sessions.
💼
Employee Assistance Program (EAP): Many employers offer 6 or more free therapy sessions annually. Check with your HR department — it's a benefit you may already have.

Mental health parity laws (Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act) require most insurers to cover mental health benefits at the same level as medical benefits. This calculator provides estimates only. Actual costs vary by provider, plan, and location. Always verify coverage directly with your insurance provider.
